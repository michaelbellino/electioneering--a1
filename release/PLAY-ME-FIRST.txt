=========================================================

RUN IT
------
Keep both .exe files together, then double-click  Stateline.exe
SmartScreen ("Windows protected your PC") -> More info -> Run anyway.
If no window appears, run Stateline.console.exe and send me what it prints.

NEW IN 0.3.0 — THE PRECISE PLATFORM
-----------------------------------
Your platform is now 24 CONCRETE POLICY STANCES, not 8 vague sliders
(the deeper system from the old browser build, rebuilt properly).

 * Open any issue area on step 3 to reveal the named policies beneath it —
   "Universal background checks", "Drug price negotiation", "Carbon pricing",
   "Sentencing reform", "Border enforcement", and 19 more.
 * Each policy has its own WEIGHT inside the area, so precise positions
   actually move your area position (shown live).
 * Each policy has a POLLING OFFSET: specific positions poll differently from
   the area they belong to. Universal background checks run 30 points ahead of
   "gun control"; defunding police runs 25 behind "criminal-justice reform".
   The chips tell you which is which, so you can be popular AND specific.
 * An IDEOLOGY COMPASS plots you on fiscal x social axes, computed from all
   24 stances — with a gold crosshair showing where THIS district's voters sit.
   Your job is to decide how close you want to stand to them.
 * Quick presets (Progressive / Centrist / Conservative) set everything at
   once if you'd rather tune from a starting point than build from scratch.
 * "Fit with race" now scores you policy-by-policy, weighting each group by
   size and by how much they care.

THE LOOP
--------
Build a candidate -> spend action points each week on ads, rallies, the ground
game and fundraisers -> survive the weekly dilemmas -> election night.
You start as an underdog nobody has heard of: buy name recognition before you
try to persuade anyone. Name recognition gates everything.

The (hamburger) button in HQ saves; "Continue" on the title screen reloads.
Settings (volume / music / reduced motion) are on the title screen.
