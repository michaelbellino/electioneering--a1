STATELINE: CAMPAIGN TRAIL — Alpha 0.1.0
========================================

A deep, animated US political CAMPAIGN simulation game.
Create a candidate, pick a real race, run the trail, and play out election night.

HOW TO RUN (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)
----------------------------------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
2. Double-click  Stateline.exe

Windows SmartScreen note:
Because this alpha isn't code-signed, Windows may show a blue
"Windows protected your PC" box. Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
(It's an unsigned indie build, not malware.)

CONTROLS
--------
Mouse for everything. Hover any button, stat, or staffer for a tooltip.
The ☰ button (top-right of HQ) saves your game. "Continue" on the title
screen reloads it.

THE LOOP
--------
1. Build your candidate: portrait, party, attributes (point-buy), a
   background trait or two, where you stand on 8 issues, and which race.
2. At HQ each week you get Action Points. Spend them on ads, rallies,
   the ground game, fundraisers, speeches and polls.
3. Watch the polls move, survive the weekly dilemmas, hire staff, and
   track the opposition on the Race Wire.
4. When the weeks run out: Election Night. Precincts report live, the
   needle swings, and the race is called.

TIP: You start as the underdog — nobody knows your name yet. Buy name
recognition before you try to persuade anyone.

Settings (volume, music, reduced motion) are on the title screen.
Made with Godot. Figures for real places are stylized approximations,
not exact Census data.
